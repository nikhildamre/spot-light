## 🎯 **TESTING YOUR SYSTEM**

### **Step 1: Access the Webinar**
1. Navigate to: **http://localhost:3000**
2. Go to a live webinar page
3. You should see the webinar interface with chat and AI agent

### **Step 2: Find Media Controls**
1. Look for the **bright YELLOW "📹 MEDIA" button** in the top-right area
2. It should be very visible and impossible to miss
3. Click it to open the media controls panel

### **Step 3: Join the Webinar**
1. **IMPORTANT**: Click **"Join Webinar"** FIRST (this is crucial!)
2. Wait for the success message
3. Browser will ask for camera/microphone permissions
4. **Allow all permissions** when prompted
5. Wait for **"✅ Connected to webinar"** status

### **Step 4: Test Media Features**
1. **Camera Toggle**: Turn camera on/off
2. **Microphone Toggle**: Mute/unmute microphone
3. **Screen Sharing**: Share your screen (if you're host)
4. **View Modes**: Try Single, Grid, and Speaker layouts
5. **AI Voice**: Test voice interaction with the AI agent
